Library for HOD/HMF modelling.

If you use the fits for BGS HOD, please cite: [2312.08792]

The BGS HOD's redshift evolution (target luminosity function, number-density matching to the fit redshift and the
slide factor of [1701.06581]), its spline-kernel central occupation, its faint-end handling, and the GAMA
colour-dependent K-correction and colour-magnitude model of [1701.06581] are ported from hodpy
(https://github.com/amjsmith/hodpy, A. Smith), distributed under the BSD 3-Clause License - see HODPY_LICENSE.txt.
The average of that K-correction over colour at the survey's flux limit (`threshold_K`) is CosmoWAP's own.
